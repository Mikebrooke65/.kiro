# Slide 1 — Shielding

## Lesson Name
Shielding

## Session 1 — Ball Mastery & Juggling
Time: 15 minutes

### Session 1 Objectives
- Make high intensity and engaging
- Short, sharp bursts of dribbling / skill work

### Session 1 Focus
- Ball mastery skills
- Turns
- Juggling variations
- Max speed dribbling

### Session 1 Coaching Points
- Eyes up
- On toes
- Acceleration with turns/fakes

## Session 2 — Shark Attack
Time: 10 minutes

### Session 2 Objectives
- Introduce shielding
- Create pressure and awareness
- Prepare for 1v1 shielding drill

### Session 2 Focus
- Protecting the ball
- Scanning
- Quick reactions

### Session 2 Coaching Points
- Introduce shielding
- Same principles as next drill

## Session 3 — 1 v 1 Shielding
Time: 15 minutes

### Session 3 Objectives
- Teach shielding technique
- Build physicality and ball protection habits

### Session 3 Focus
- Body between ball and defender
- Open body shape
- Switching ball to safe foot

### Session 3 Coaching Points
- Shield by getting your body between the ball and defender
- Open body (see both ball and player)
- Adjust and switch ball to other foot when defender comes ball‑side

## Session 4 — Game
Time: 20 minutes

### Session 4 Objectives
- Apply shielding in game context
- Encourage team movement and transitions

### Session 4 Focus
- 40×30 field
- Halfway rule: entire team must be over halfway to score

### Session 4 Coaching Points
- Movement
- Support angles
- Decision making

## Notes
None
